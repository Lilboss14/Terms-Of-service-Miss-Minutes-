# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** 31 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this Discord bot handles data.

## Data We Collect

The bot may collect and store:

* Discord User IDs
* Server IDs
* Channel IDs
* Messages or command inputs (only when required for bot functionality)
* Configuration and settings for servers

## How We Use Data

The collected data is used only to:

* Provide the bot's features and commands.
* Save server settings and user preferences.
* Improve the bot's performance and reliability.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties unless required by law.

## Data Security

Reasonable steps are taken to protect stored data, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## Data Removal

If you would like your stored data removed, please contact the bot developer. Data required for core bot functionality may be retained where necessary.

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time. Continued use of the bot after changes means you accept the updated Privacy Policy.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the bot developer.
